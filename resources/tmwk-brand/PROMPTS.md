# Image generation prompts

Tool: built-in imagegen, with `transparent_background: true` for every extraction
and cleanup. The input was the approved Builder concept sheet from this conversation.
No Rex artwork is included in these assets.

## Mascot extraction

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied approved tmwk beaver
concept sheet. Extract the SINGLE standalone beaver from the upper right of the
sheet into a clean high-resolution transparent PNG master mascot. Preserve the
approved creature EXACTLY: blue-to-mint smooth body, two tiny rounded ears, dark
oval eyes, cream two-lobed muzzle, two white buckteeth, little front paws, two
rounded feet, and large purple paddle tail with one diagonal groove. Preserve all
proportions, pose, expression, colors, gradient shading and three-quarter viewing
angle. Do not redesign or reinterpret. Only the beaver, centered, with all ears,
feet and entire tail in frame and about 6% clear margin. Genuinely transparent
background and transparent outside the character. Remove the floor/contact
shadow, board, tiles, all text and all other examples. Square canvas at high
resolution. No wordmark or letters.

## Mascot edge cleanup

Production cleanup of the supplied transparent blue beaver mascot. Preserve the
character design, proportions, all colors and smooth shading exactly. Remove all
the detached blue and cyan flecks, streaks, fringe and tiny pixels around the
creature, especially to the right of the purple tail and between its tail and
right ear. Repair the one ragged notch on its left shoulder under the muzzle with
a smooth continuous curve. Produce a clean smooth antialiased contour around one
beaver with no disconnected pixels outside it. Entire background fully
transparent. No shadows, no floor, no outlines, no text, no tile. Same centered
full-body beaver, complete tail, square transparent PNG.

## App-icon extraction

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the supplied approved tmwk beaver brand
concept sheet. Deliver one production macOS desktop app icon, a square
high-resolution PNG, with GENUINE transparency outside the rounded-square tile.
Isolate and faithfully reproduce the LARGE LEFT icon in the reference: softly
beveled pearl-white rounded-square tile, very subtle lilac light, same mint-to-blue
beaver body, tiny rounded ears, oval dark eyes, cream two-lobed muzzle, two white
buckteeth, tiny paws and rounded feet, oversized purple paddle tail with one
diagonal groove. Preserve the approved beaver proportions, expression, colors,
tail position and pose EXACTLY. No redesign. Tile front-facing, perfectly square,
no perspective, about 82% of square canvas width with equal transparent margins all
sides, substantial continuous macOS rounded corners. Keep soft tile shading and a
very subtle short shadow within margins. The character fills roughly 80% tile
width like the reference. Remove the concept board, text, labels, all other
examples and wordmark. ONE icon only centered on transparent square canvas. No
outside opaque white background. No text, no badge, no watermark.

## App-icon edge cleanup

Edit target: supplied beaver application icon. Change ONLY the outer edge and
background transparency. Keep the beaver and entire interior of the pearl
rounded-square tile exactly unchanged. Remove ALL stray white pixels, speckles,
fringe, glows and shadows outside the tile. The tile must have a perfectly clean
smooth antialiased continuous rounded-square contour, with the empty margins fully
transparent. No external drop shadow at all. No white fuzzy fringe. Preserve same
tile size, same square canvas, same beaver pose and colors. This is production icon
cleanup, not a redesign. One centered icon only, transparent square PNG.

## Final app-icon perimeter

Create the final production version of this exact beaver macOS app icon. Preserve
the beaver inside the tile unchanged. Fix the app tile's outline: give the
pearl-white rounded square a continuous visible fine dark muted-purple perimeter
stroke, 3 pixels thick at 1024px resolution. This stroke must completely enclose
the tile and separate it from the empty transparent surroundings. ALL pixels
outside that neat perimeter are fully transparent with no speckles or glows. No
exterior shadow or fringe. Four beautifully smooth rounded corners. A single
perfectly square front-facing tile centered in a square canvas with transparent
margins, same overall sizing as input. Retain the pearl-white interior and subtle
lilac shading. Preserve mint-blue beaver, cream muzzle and two buckteeth, dark eyes,
and large purple paddle tail exactly. ONE icon no text no other objects.
