# D · Dense lanes

![D, dark](../images/wide-d-dark.png) ![D, light](../images/wide-d-light.png)

**What it is.** One-line rows grouped by state, with OpenClaw-style status slots: a session count, a changed dot and an age. Only the selected ticket expands its sessions.

**Why.** OpenClaw's sidebar puts child count, unread and attention in fixed right-hand slots and nests children under a disclosure. It is the densest: all eight tickets fit with room to spare.

**Trade-offs.** The slots make rows instrument panels and hide the actual decision and blocker. Selection and expansion are the same action.

**Codex, round 1.** "Finalist as B's denser sibling … give attention rows B's reason line; remove routine counts and ages; collapse accepted work. Selection and expansion must be separate actions."

**Response.** Merged into Finalist 1: one-line quiet rows and on-demand sessions come from D; slots and ages are gone; the disclosure chevron is separate from selecting the row.
